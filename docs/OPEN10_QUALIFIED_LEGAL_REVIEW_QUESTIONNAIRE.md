## 2. Instructions For Reviewers

1. Complete only the questions within the reviewer's documented competence and authority.
2. Keep repository fact, hypothetical assumption, reviewer response, and evidence reference separate. Do not rewrite a repository fact as a legal conclusion.
3. Use `NOT PROVIDED` when evidence or a response has not been supplied, `NOT SELECTED` when an entity, provider, location, owner, or option has not been chosen, and `NOT DETERMINED` when qualified analysis has not reached a conclusion.
4. Cite durable primary evidence for each answer. Identify document title, issuing authority, version or date, scope, and stable reference without copying sensitive content into this questionnaire.
5. Record assumptions, exclusions, conflicts, limitations, effective dates, transition periods, and unresolved questions explicitly.
6. Do not infer that consent is required or sufficient, that a legal instrument applies, or that a regulator has authority. Those are reviewer determinations requiring evidence.
7. Do not insert production credentials, patient information, workforce identifiers, confidential contract text, or legal advice that is not approved for repository publication.
8. An answer does not approve an OPEN decision or pass a production gate. Decision authority must record a separate dated outcome with evidence.
9. Where applicability is undetermined, answer in two stages: first determine whether a duty, process, contract, control, right, notification, retention rule, or other requirement applies and cite qualified evidence; only then, if applicable, define its scope, implementation, owner, evidence, exceptions, and review conditions.
10. Questionnaire completion and decision outcome are independent. Incomplete evidence prevents `APPROVE`, but it does not prevent a properly evidenced `REVISE` or `REJECT` outcome.
