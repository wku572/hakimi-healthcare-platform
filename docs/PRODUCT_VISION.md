# Product Vision

Hakimi / ሀኪሜ is a proposed healthcare appointment platform for Ethiopia.

This vision remains **PROPOSED**. It is the product direction the repository is exploring, not a confirmed owner-approved baseline.

## Intended Direction

- Help patients discover and register with healthcare facilities.
- Help facilities manage practitioner rosters and appointment capacity.
- Support appointment scheduling, cancellation, and reminder workflows.
- Keep the foundation strict, typed, and operationally simple.
- Preserve the DevOps learning focus by keeping migrations, verification, and deployment mechanics explicit.

## Scope Boundaries

- No authentication or authorization baseline is confirmed yet.
- No clinical record system is defined in the current baseline.
- No payment, claims, or insurance workflow is included.
- No recurring appointment engine is included.
- No queue broker, Redis layer, or notification transport is confirmed.
