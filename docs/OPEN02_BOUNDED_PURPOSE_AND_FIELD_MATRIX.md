# OPEN-02 Bounded Purpose And Field Matrix

Status: `HYPOTHETICAL`, `SYNTHETIC DATA ONLY`, `NOT APPROVED FOR PRODUCTION`

Production deployment: `NOT AUTHORIZED`

Real patient-data processing: `NOT AUTHORIZED`

Canonical decision type: `OPEN DECISION`

Recorded product-owner outcome: `REVISE`

Implementation/governance selection: `PENDING`

Sprint 17 remediation status: `S17-OPEN02-01` remains `IN PROGRESS`; `S17-OPEN02-07` remains `IN PROGRESS`.

This matrix develops repository-backed evidence for [OPEN-02](./REQUIREMENTS.md) remediation. It does not approve a privacy policy, lawful basis, consent model, notice, rights workflow, patient self-service, production deployment, or real patient-data processing. It does not complete or check any Sprint 17 issue item.

## Interpretation Rules

- `CURRENT REPOSITORY FACT` means behavior, interface, schema, migration, access policy, or test evidence present in the repository.
- `PROPOSED PURPOSE` means a bounded purpose interpretation prepared for OPEN-02 review; it is not approved policy.
- `TECHNICAL ACCESS` means the implemented route, OIDC resource-server boundary, role grant, facility scope, relationship rule, field allowlist, or blocked policy state.
- `NOT DETERMINED` means the repository does not establish the organizational, legal, privacy, clinical, facility, or production fact.
- `REQUIRED QUALIFIED REVIEW` means evidence needed from accountable product, privacy, legal, clinical safety, records-management, operations, security, or patient-administration reviewers before policy approval or implementation.
- The portfolio/demo and synthetic-data-only posture is a product boundary, not a lawful ground or privacy approval.
- Public health interfaces are repository interfaces; live callers, production monitors, providers, and locations are `NOT DETERMINED`.
- Workforce-protected interfaces are implemented for synthetic data only; patient authentication, patient self-service, production deployment, and real patient-data processing remain unauthorized.
