## Interpretation Rules

- `CURRENT REPOSITORY FACT` means behavior, documentation, tests, or interfaces visible in this repository. It does not prove deployment, live traffic, real users, an operating entity, a controller, a processor, a selected provider, a selected jurisdiction, or real patient-data processing.
- `PROPOSED CONTROL` means a reviewable candidate for `OPEN-02`; it is not selected or approved.
- `HYPOTHETICAL ASSUMPTION` means a possible future operating assumption; approval requires replacing it with evidence.
- `NOT DETERMINED` means the repository does not establish the value.
- `REQUIRED EXTERNAL EVIDENCE` means evidence from product-owner, privacy, legal, clinical, security, records, operations, or facility review outside the current codebase.
