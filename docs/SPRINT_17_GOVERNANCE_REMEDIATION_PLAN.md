## Recommended Evidence-Development Sequence

This is a recommended evidence-development sequence. It is not an approval
dependency that prevents a properly evidenced `REVISE` or `REJECT` outcome for any
individual decision.

1. `OPEN-12` establishes operating-entity, deployment-context, ownership, and
   operational facts.
2. `OPEN-10` evaluates legal and regulatory applicability using genuine operating
   facts.
3. `OPEN-02` defines privacy purposes, lawful grounds, notices, consent rules
   where applicable, patient rights, and minimum-necessary handling.
4. `OPEN-08` defines patient identity, duplicate handling, stewardship,
   merge/unmerge, representative authority, and cross-facility ownership.
5. `OPEN-07` defines retention, deletion, backup, restoration, and legal-hold rules
   using applicable legal, privacy, identity, and operational inputs.
6. `OPEN-06` defines security and clinical audit requirements using the approved
   identity, privacy, retention, legal, and operational boundaries.

Work may proceed concurrently where evidence is available. Cross-decision
references require reconciliation of relevant evidence, not completion or approval
of the referenced decision as a whole.

`OPEN-06` and `OPEN-07` relationships to `OPEN-12` are downstream reconciliation
and coordination points. They are not prerequisites for collecting `OPEN-12`
operating facts such as ownership, service objectives, recovery objectives,
monitoring ownership, or change authority.

## Remediation Register

Allowed status values are `NOT STARTED`, `IN PROGRESS`, `READY FOR REVIEW`, and
`BLOCKED`. Every named owner is `NOT ASSIGNED` unless repository evidence names
one. No row records approval or production authorization.

Dependency and coordination notation:

- A full remediation ID, such as `S17-OPEN12-04`, identifies a specific evidence
  input or coordination point.
- A range, such as `S17-OPEN12-01 through S17-OPEN12-10`, identifies relevant
  outputs from the rows in that inclusive range.
- An aggregate notation, such as `S17-OPEN12`, identifies relevant evidence outputs
  from that decision's remediation set. It does not require every row in that
  decision to be complete before work begins.
- A phrase such as `OPEN-12 remediation` has the same aggregate meaning as
  `S17-OPEN12`.
- Cross-decision references are iterative reconciliation points, not mandatory
  workflow prerequisites, approval dependencies, or requirements that the referenced
  decision be completed first.
